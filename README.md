# 📊 Statistical Analysis of Factors Influencing Subject Choice Among Graduate-Level Students

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes the factors influencing subject selection among graduate-level students at Shri Shivaji Science College, Amravati, during the academic year 2024–2025.

The study explores students' academic preferences, career aspirations, college selection factors, and demographic characteristics using statistical analysis and data visualization.

##  Objectives

* Identify the major factors influencing students' subject choices.
* Analyze students' academic and career preferences.
* Examine relationships between selected variables using statistical tests.
* Present findings through tables, charts, and graphs.

##  Tools and Techniques

* Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets
* Descriptive Statistics
* Frequency and Percentage Analysis
* Chi-Square Test
* Spearman's Rank Correlation
* Data Visualization

##  Key Findings

* Computer Science was the most preferred first-choice subject in the study.
* Scoring potential was reported as an important factor in subject selection.
* College facilities and environment influenced college selection.
* Government-sector careers were the most popular career preference in the survey.

##  Project Workflow

1. Collect survey responses using Google Forms.
2. Organize and clean the data.
3. Calculate frequencies and percentages.
4. Conduct statistical hypothesis testing.
5. Create charts and graphs.
6. Interpret the results and draw conclusions.
